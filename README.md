# ListingLift

ListingLift is a conversion-focused listing growth system for Etsy and Amazon Handmade sellers. Customers submit their shop data, product photos, competitor examples, and target keywords; ListingLift delivers optimized titles, descriptions, tags, image-order recommendations, pricing insights, and a prioritized test plan within 48 hours. A free ListingLift Scorecard and before-and-after teardown create a shareable viral hook, while measurable experiments and monthly refreshes turn one-time optimization into recurring growth. Results are positioned as improvement opportunities rather than guaranteed rankings or sales.

**Category:** E-commerce
**Target Audience:** Independent Etsy and Amazon Handmade sellers with 10-200 listings who are struggling with low traffic or stalled sales
**Revenue Model:** Use a monetization ladder: free ListingLift Scorecard and public teardown contest for lead generation; $29 single-listing audit; $79 optimized listing package; $199 five-listing Growth Sprint; $399 ten-listing Conversion System; and $149-$499 monthly subscriptions for ongoing refreshes, keyword monitoring, seasonal updates, experiment tracking, and new listings. Add a $49 expedited delivery fee, agency/reseller plans, and performance bonuses tied to agreed-upon measurable deliverables rather than guaranteed revenue. Encourage referrals with a free listing refresh or account credit.
